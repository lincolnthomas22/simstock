## About This Game

> **Start with $1,000 and a browser full of strangers' companies.**
>
> SimStock is a stock market game for people who have never bought a share and
> would quite like to understand what one is. Research a company, look at what
> it earns, decide whether the price makes sense, and buy — or don't. One
> second in the game is one trading day, and you can pause that or run it at
> four times speed while you think.
>
> **The market has reasons.**
>
> Prices are not random walks with a nice chart on top. Every company has
> profits behind it, a fair value those profits imply, a P/E ratio, quarterly
> earnings on the calendar and dividends that land in your cash three weeks
> after each report. There is a market index that everything moves with, booms
> and recessions that come and go, and news that moves a price for a reason you
> can read.
>
> **Find out whether you are actually any good.**
>
> The portfolio screen charts your net worth against the market itself, which
> is the only honest scoreboard: beating the index is the whole game, and
> paying commission to keep up with it is the trap.
>
> **Then grow the desk.**
>
> Hire staff for income that arrives whether or not you are trading. Unlock six
> account tiers, each opening bigger and rougher parts of the board — from
> steady famous names through crypto and, if you insist, memecoins.
>
> **Or race someone.**
>
> The Versus room is 1v1: one stock, $1,000 each, a risk level and a length
> chosen by the host, and whoever is worth more when the bell goes wins. The
> chart switches between what the stock is doing and who is winning. Your
> career game is sealed off from it entirely — a match cannot cost you
> anything. There is a practice opponent on the desk when nobody else is
> around.
>
> **39 achievements**, a save that follows you between machines, and a game
> that runs the same on a laptop as on a handheld.
>
> Every company, coin and price in SimStock is fictional and simulated. The
> names are parodies. No real money is involved, and nothing here is investment
> advice.
